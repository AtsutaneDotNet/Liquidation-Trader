Advance Strategy Settings

Circuit Breaker Strategy

Halts new entries when extreme volatility is detected based on ATR multiples and volume surges.

Skip trade if price moved more than this % over lookback period.

VWAP Strategy

Calculates a rolling or session-based Volume-Weighted Average Price. Triggers LONG when price falls below the lower offset, and SHORT when price rises above the upper offset.

RSI Strategy

Uses Relative Strength Index to avoid entries in exhausted zones. Triggers LONG when oversold, and SHORT when overbought.

DMI Strategy

Evaluates trend strength. Trade is blocked if DMI is above the threshold (ensuring mean reversion entries only). Direction dictated by DI components.

Market Sentiment Strategy (🌟 Premium)

Leverages global sentiment index. Buys when sentiment is Bullish + Fear/Greed; sells when Bearish + Fear/Greed. Uses Extreme Fear/Greed triggers. Requires RapidAPI Key.
